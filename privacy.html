Legal information & privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

How STILL handles information. STILL has no accounts and no server. Your breathing practice and mood check-ins stay on your device.

1. Who is responsible

STILL (the “App”) is a breathing and mindfulness app published by El Abdellaoui Meriame (the “Publisher”), an individual developer based in France. For the limited information described in sections 8 to 10, the Publisher is the data controller (responsable de traitement) under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Contact for every privacy question or request: teladanoe@gmail.com. Full publisher details are in the Legal Notice.

This policy applies to the Android app and to the web version of STILL, and it explains how the legal pages themselves are hosted (section 15).

2. In short

  • There are no accounts. STILL never asks for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Your mood check-ins, breathing history, preferences, and sanctuary progress are stored on your device only. The Publisher has no server and no database of users, and cannot see them.
  • STILL contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service of its own, and no tracking.
  • Android’s automatic backup is switched off for STILL, so Google does not copy STILL’s data to your Google account.
  • Supporting STILL is optional. On Android, Google Play processes the payment. On the web, Buy Me a Coffee and its payment provider Stripe process it. Payment information is separate from your wellness data.

3. Two kinds of information

STILL deals with two different kinds of information. They are handled separately.

Wellness data on your device

Mood check-ins, breathing history, sanctuary progress, preferences, and reminder settings. They are created on your device and stay there. They are never sent to the Publisher or to anyone else by STILL. Because the Publisher cannot access them, the Publisher does not process them as a controller.

Mood check-ins can say something about how you feel. That is why STILL keeps them on your device and gives you Export data and Delete everything.

Payment and contact information

If you choose to support STILL, or if you write to the Publisher, some information reaches the Publisher through Google Play, Buy Me a Coffee, or email (sections 8 and 9). This is the only information about you that the Publisher can see, and it never includes your check-ins, sessions, or preferences.

4. Wellness data stored on your device

STILL saves the following in its private app storage on Android, or in your browser’s local storage on the web:

  • Mood check-ins (the mood you choose and the time).
  • Your breathing history (date, length, technique, and the mood if you chose one) and your total mindful minutes.
  • Your sanctuary (garden) progress, calculated from your completed sessions.
  • Your answers during the introduction.
  • Your preferences: breathing technique, duration, appearance, motion, sound, volume, cues, spoken guidance, and breath holding.
  • Your reminder settings.

The App also keeps generated ambient sound files in its cache. They contain no personal information.

Android backup

STILL turns off Android’s backup and restore for its data. Google does not copy STILL’s data to your Google account, and the data is not carried to a new phone. Your progress is lost if you uninstall the App or change phone, unless you export it first.

5. Reminders and notifications

If you turn reminders on, your device schedules local notifications. The text is generated on the device. On Android 13 and later, STILL asks your permission before it shows notifications. STILL declares the boot-completed permission so that Android can restore your scheduled reminders after a restart.

STILL does not use push notifications from a server and does not request a push token. Its notification library contains Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging component. STILL contains no Firebase project configuration, and the component’s automatic initialization is switched off. STILL’s own code sends nothing to Firebase.

6. Third-party components

STILL is built with open-source libraries (Expo and React Native) that run on your device. Fonts and sounds are bundled with the App and are not downloaded.

On Android, two Google components are present: the Google Play Billing library, used only if you choose to support STILL, and the Firebase Cloud Messaging library described in section 5. STILL contains no advertising, analytics, attribution, or social-media SDK.

Google Play and Google Play services on your device work with Google under Google’s own terms, whether or not you use STILL. See Google Privacy Policy.

7. Android permissions

STILL requests no sensitive permission. The release build declares:

  • Notifications and boot completion: to show your reminders and restore them after a restart.
  • Vibration: for gentle haptic feedback while you breathe.
  • Internet and network state: declared by system and billing components. STILL’s own code does not send your wellness data over the network.
  • Wake lock and audio settings: for uninterrupted sound playback and keeping the screen on during a session.
  • Google Play billing: to process an optional contribution.

STILL does not request the microphone, camera, location, contacts, calendar, storage, or advertising-ID permissions.

8. Optional support and payments

Supporting STILL is voluntary. A contribution unlocks nothing, and there is no subscription.

On Android: Google Play

Payment is processed by Google under Google’s Terms of Service for Google Play and Google Privacy Policy. STILL does not receive or store your payment card details. STILL receives only the outcome of the purchase (completed, pending, cancelled, or failed) and a purchase token needed to complete it. The App does not store this token and does not send it anywhere else.

On the web: Buy Me a Coffee and Stripe

The support button opens a Buy Me a Coffee page in a new browser tab. Buy Me a Coffee processes the payment and uses Stripe for card payments, each under its own policy: Buy Me a Coffee Privacy Policy and Stripe Privacy Policy. STILL sends them none of your check-ins, sessions, or preferences. Buy Me a Coffee states that supporter names and messages can appear on the creator’s public page by default, so check your settings on that service before you write a message.

What the Publisher can see about a contribution

The Publisher sees the contribution records that the payment provider makes available to the page owner:

  • Google Play: the details Google shows developers in Play Console, for example the order number, product, amount, currency, date, and country.
  • Buy Me a Coffee: the name or display name you entered, your email address, your message if you wrote one, the amount, and the date. It never shows the Publisher your full card number.
  • Neither service gives the Publisher access to anything stored in STILL.

The Publisher uses these records to manage contributions, to thank supporters where appropriate, and to meet accounting and tax obligations. For these records only, the Publisher is a data controller.

9. Messages you send to the Publisher

If you write to teladanoe@gmail.com, the Publisher receives your email address and the content of your message, and uses them only to answer you and to handle your request. Please do not send health details you do not want to share.

10. What the Publisher can and cannot access

  • Cannot access: your mood check-ins, breathing history, sanctuary progress, preferences, and reminder settings. They never leave your device unless you export them yourself.
  • Can access: contribution records (section 8), messages you send (section 9), and the reviews you publish on Google Play.
  • Aggregated statistics: Google Play Console shows the Publisher aggregated and anonymous figures such as installs and ratings. Google can also share crash and performance reports from users who have chosen to share diagnostics with Google. STILL contains no crash-reporting service of its own.

11. Purposes and legal bases

  • Managing and recording contributions: the Publisher’s legal obligations (accounting and tax) under article 6(1)(c) GDPR, and the Publisher’s legitimate interest in managing and thanking supporters under article 6(1)(f).
  • Answering your messages and requests: the Publisher’s legitimate interest in replying to you (article 6(1)(f)), and, for requests to exercise your rights, the Publisher’s legal obligation (article 6(1)(c)).
  • Serving this legal website securely: the Publisher’s legitimate interest in providing the pages (article 6(1)(f)). GitHub processes the technical logs (section 15).

Providing information to the Publisher is voluntary. If you do not provide it, the only consequence is that the Publisher cannot answer you or acknowledge your contribution. STILL makes no automated decision about you and does no profiling. STILL relies on no consent, because it collects nothing that requires it.

12. Recipients and international transfers

The Publisher does not sell personal data and shares contribution records and messages with no one, except as the law requires.

Google, Buy Me a Coffee, Stripe, and GitHub act under their own policies and may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. For transfers from the European Union, GitHub and Buy Me a Coffee state in their policies that they rely on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses (GitHub also on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework). See each provider’s policy for its safeguards: Google, Buy Me a Coffee, Stripe, GitHub.

13. Retention

  • Wellness data on your device stays until you delete it in the App, clear the App’s or the browser’s data, or uninstall the App.
  • Contribution records: kept for as long as the accounting and tax obligations that apply to the Publisher require. The Publisher sets no other retention period. Google, Buy Me a Coffee, and Stripe keep their own records under their own policies.
  • Messages to the Publisher: kept for as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up, then deleted unless a legal obligation requires keeping them.

14. Export and deletion

Control names below are shown exactly as they appear on screen in the App.

Export

Nothing leaves your device automatically. If you tap Export data in Settings, STILL prepares a text copy of your records (preferences, sessions, check-ins, and totals) and opens your device’s share menu. You choose the destination. STILL writes no file, so no temporary copy is left behind. The receiving app handles the data under its own policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything STILL keeps, with no account and no internet connection:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Under Your data, choose Delete everything.
  3. Confirm.

This cancels your scheduled reminders and deletes your mood check-ins, breathing history, sanctuary progress, introduction answers, and preferences, then returns STILL to its first-launch state. Uninstalling the App on Android also removes its data. Copies you made yourself, such as an exported text, are outside STILL.

15. Web version, cookies, and this website

The web version of STILL sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising, or third-party scripts. It uses your browser’s local storage to keep your data on that browser. This storage is strictly necessary for the service you request, so no consent banner is shown. You can clear it with Delete everything or in your browser settings.

This legal website is static. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising, fonts, or third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of visitors for security, whether or not they have a GitHub account, and the Publisher has no access to these logs. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

16. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), for any personal data the Publisher holds about you, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection, and portability. To exercise them, write to teladanoe@gmail.com. The Publisher replies within one month, which the law allows to be extended in complex cases, and may ask you to confirm your identity.

For data stored only on your device, you already hold it: use Export data and Delete everything in Settings.

You can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (Commission Nationale de l’Informatique et des Libertés), or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence. The EDPB lists these authorities.

17. Children

STILL is intended for people aged 15 and over. If you are under 15, use STILL with the agreement of a parent or guardian. Buy Me a Coffee requires its users to be 18 or over, so contributions through the web version are for adults.

18. Security

On Android, your data stays in STILL’s private storage, protected by the operating system. Protect your device with a screen lock. STILL’s own code sends no wellness data over the network, so there is nothing of that kind to intercept in transit. Payments are protected by Google, Buy Me a Coffee, and Stripe.

The Publisher may update this policy when the App changes. The date of the latest update appears at the top of this page, and significant changes are described in the App’s release notes.
